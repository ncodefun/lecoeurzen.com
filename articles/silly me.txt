silly me
thought I could explain
the mystical vision at night
though it would happen
that I would go somewhere with that
impressing the world, make my place like a star bright
and here I am, I have arrived, but nowhere to be found
arrived to just what it is, that wants to be
I open, and make it home
no strangers, no outside for the limitless
no ground to keep you safe

don't you know all that pushes and pull in the dark
that come for the crown
for the head of the king
and the balance of power
the crowds, the people
the human of you, broken into pieces
crying in the dark night
to be freed, to return empty 
to the very source of life
you
